Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ZenZone. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

ZenZone collects minimal data to function:

  • Screen Time Data: With your permission, we access Screen Time data to help you manage app usage and create focus routines. This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • User Profile: Basic information such as your name and preferences, stored locally on your device.
  • App Usage (Routines): The routines and schedules you create are stored locally and in your personal iCloud if enabled.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the ZenZone app functionality
  • Enable focus routines and app blocking features
  • Improve user experience

4. Data Storage and Security

All personal data is stored locally on your device using secure Apple frameworks (UserDefaults, App Groups). We do not operate backend servers that store your personal information. Screen Time and Family Controls data is handled entirely by Apple's operating system.

5. Third-Party Services

ZenZone does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We do not integrate third-party analytics or advertising frameworks.

6. Children's Privacy

ZenZone is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@zenzone.app.
